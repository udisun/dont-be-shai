(U)Key judgments

  1. We assess with high confidence that the selected queue advances more slowly than at least one adjacent queue, and that the disparity becomes apparent within ninety seconds of committing to a choice.

  2. We assess with high confidence that switching queues transfers the property to the new queue rather than resolving it.
