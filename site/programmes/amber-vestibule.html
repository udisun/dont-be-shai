The lift doors that close as you approach

A study of door-close timing in relation to approaching foot traffic, and why the interval shortens when the approach is hurried.

(U)Key judgments

  1. We assess with high confidence that lift doors complete closure at a measurably faster rate when a person is visibly hurrying toward them. Across observed closures, the interval contracted whenever the approaching party broke into a jog.

  2. We assess with moderate confidence that the effect is strongest when the approaching party is carrying something in both hands, and absent entirely when they are early.

(U)Observations

Bottom line

The programme is efficient, well resourced and entirely unaccountable. Occupants report that the doors reopen readily for anyone who does not need them to.
