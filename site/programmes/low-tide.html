The discount that begins after you buy

(U)Key judgments

  1. We assess with high confidence that the probability of a price reduction rises sharply in the days following a purchase, and that the reduction exceeds any available return window by a small and consistent margin.

  2. We assess with moderate confidence that the effect is amplified when the purchase was deliberated over for more than a week.
